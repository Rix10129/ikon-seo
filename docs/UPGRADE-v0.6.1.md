# Upgrade to Ikon SEO v0.6.1

This patch makes Google Business Profile integration explicitly optional.

After upgrading, open **Ikon SEO → Business Profile** and choose one option:

- **No, not yet** — hides Google OAuth setup and keeps all website SEO tools available.
- **Yes, I have one** — shows the existing Google Business Profile connection controls.

No website profile, inventory, page plans or draft content is deleted during this upgrade.
