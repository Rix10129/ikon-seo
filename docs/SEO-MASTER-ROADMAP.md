# SEO Master development roadmap

Ikon SEO is an evidence-based SEO operating system. v1.0.0 consolidated the mature modules into a Closed-Loop Operating Plan. v1.2.0 adds Structured Data and Media Governance while preserving approval-first execution and read-only external evidence collection.

## Diagnostic Accuracy (0.9.1)

- Remove arbitrary length and word-count penalties
- Semantic topic alignment without exact-match requirements
- Root-cause deduplication
- Evidence-sufficiency reporting
- Separate ranking, search-appearance, conversion and measurement findings
- Priority based on impact, confidence, business value and effort

## Phase 1 — Evidence Foundation (0.9.0)

- Same-site rendered page crawler
- Page-level technical and content evidence
- Search Console page/query mapping
- Optional Analytics landing-page evidence
- Direct facts versus inferred hypotheses
- Fix-priority queue and per-page reports
- Robots.txt and sitemap snapshots

## Phase 2 — Search intent and competitor intelligence

- Current search-result research initiated from the private operator workspace
- Competitor service and topic coverage
- Dominant result-type classification
- Page-purpose and intent mismatch diagnosis
- Differentiation requirements
- Evidence saved to Project History

Competitor research should cite live sources and remain separate from facts stored in the Website Profile.

## Phase 3 — Cannibalisation and internal-link intelligence

- Query-to-page mapping from Search Console
- Content and heading similarity
- Healthy support versus harmful overlap classification
- Exact internal-link placement suggestions
- Link-depth and topical-hub reports
- Merge, reposition and redirect recommendations

## Phase 4 — Technical experience and performance

- PageSpeed Insights and Core Web Vitals connection
- Mobile and desktop evidence
- Image, script, font and layout-shift opportunities
- Page-template and sitewide pattern detection
- Hosting and origin-response separation

## Phase 5 — Authority evidence

- Search Console links where available
- CSV imports from trusted backlink tools
- Referring-domain and destination-page mapping
- Broken backlink opportunities
- Competitor link-gap imports
- Clear limitation when reliable authority data is absent

## Phase 6 — Controlled optimisation

- Improvement drafts generated from approved evidence
- Metadata and snippet proposals
- Internal-link insertion drafts
- Schema and redirect approval queues
- Image review queue
- Page consolidation and rollback workflows

No live change should occur without explicit administrator approval.

## Phase 7 — Outcome learning

- Before-and-after evidence snapshots
- 14, 30, 60 and 90-day comparisons
- Recommendation success and failure records
- Content-decay monitoring
- Branded client reports
- Site-specific decision history

Outcome history improves future recommendations for the connected website; it does not claim to reproduce Google's private ranking systems.
