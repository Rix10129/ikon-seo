# Publisher Intelligence

Ikon SEO v0.16.0 adds an approval-first publishing system for local-business, editorial, affiliate, advertising, ecommerce and hybrid websites.

## Core records

- Keyword and opportunity universe
- Topic hubs
- Editorial calendar and pipeline
- Author and reviewer profiles
- Source and evidence requirements
- Quality-gate evidence
- Privacy-preserving portfolio signatures
- Content lifecycle recommendations

## Pipeline stages

Idea → Research → Planned → Brief → Approved → Drafting → Review → Revision → Ready → Published → Refresh

Consolidation and retirement are separate review states. They never modify live pages automatically.

## Quality gate

The gate combines target alignment, same-site originality, portfolio-signature similarity, sources, authorship, review ownership, internal links and disclosure requirements. A passed result means ready for human review; it is not permission to publish automatically.

## Portfolio safeguards

Signature bundles contain hashed content fingerprints, URLs, titles and short topic terms. They do not contain full article text. Import signature bundles from other managed websites to detect excessive overlap before publication.

## Lifecycle decisions

Recommendations are evidence-led and may include keep, update, consolidate, redirect review or retirement review. Search visibility, modification age and duplication evidence are considered, but a human must verify intent, conversions, links and business value before any consequential action.
