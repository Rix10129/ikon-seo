Campaign scope

One growth system. Six connected workstreams.

The scope should follow the constraint. A site with indexing problems needs a different first month from a site with strong technical foundations but weak commercial pages, local visibility or authority.

01 / Technical SEO

Build a foundation search engines can use.

Crawlability, indexation, canonicals, structured data, XML sitemaps, rendering, performance, migrations and implementation support.

02 / Commercial pages

Map demand to pages built to satisfy it.

Priority services, categories and locations; intent matching; SERP presentation; UX and conversion-oriented structure.

03 / Content

Answer the questions that support buying decisions.

Research, useful expert-led content, supporting resources, information gain, topical coverage and content refreshes.

04 / Internal architecture

Make priority pages easier to discover and understand.

Hub design, contextual linking, crawl paths, priority-page reinforcement, orphan detection and cannibalization control.

05 / Authority

Strengthen the signals competitive pages need.

Relevant link earning, digital PR opportunities, brand mentions, local citations where applicable and source-quality review.

06 / Measurement

Keep the next decision tied to evidence.

Search Console, analytics, rankings or local visibility where useful, implementation history, page-level evidence and business context.

Dubai-specific strategy

Dubai search demand is competitive, local and commercially fragmented.

A Dubai campaign should not be built by swapping a city name into generic pages. Search intent can differ by service, audience, neighborhood, device, Maps visibility and competitor set. The work begins by mapping commercial demand to the pages and surfaces most likely to earn it.

A

Map service × location demand

Separate genuine location intent from unnecessary doorway-page expansion.

B

Audit the Dubai SERP

Compare who ranks, what page type wins, what evidence is visible and where commercial gaps exist.

C

Control cannibalization

Keep the homepage, SEO Services, Local SEO, Technical SEO and AI Search pages responsible for distinct intent.

Reporting

SEO should not be a black box.

Reporting should show what changed, why it changed, what happened afterward and what deserves attention next. Rankings matter, but they are only one layer of the story.

What should be visible

  • Priority pages and work completed
  • Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR and page/query movement
  • Analytics engagement and conversion data when available
  • Local visibility where relevant
  • Technical health and indexation
  • Authority and mention progress

What the report should answer

  • What did we change?
  • What evidence justified the change?
  • What happened after implementation?
  • What is still holding growth back?
  • What should be done next?
  • What should be stopped or deprioritized?

Common questions

Before you start.

How much do SEO services in Dubai cost?

There is no responsible single price before the scope is understood. The required investment depends on the site, competition, technical condition, number of priority pages, local requirements, content and authority work, and how much implementation is handled by Ikon. Start with an opportunity review so the scope is based on evidence rather than a generic package.

How long does SEO take?

SEO is not a fixed-time ranking guarantee. Some technical or on-page improvements can be implemented quickly, while competitive commercial growth usually requires ongoing crawling, indexing, content, authority and iteration. The starting condition and market determine the timeline.

What is included in your SEO service?

The scope can include technical SEO, priority landing pages, content, internal linking, local search, authority, measurement and implementation support. The campaign should prioritize what is holding growth back rather than forcing every client into the same checklist.

Do you handle Google Maps and local SEO?

Yes, when local discovery is relevant to the business. Local SEO is treated as a distinct workstream involving Google Business Profile, location relevance, reviews, citations and geographic visibility.

Do you optimize for AI search?

Ikon can improve the underlying signals that support discoverability across AI-driven search experiences: clear entity information, useful source material, brand mentions, topical coverage and citation opportunities. No agency can guarantee that a particular AI system will cite or mention a website.

Do you create content?

Yes when content is part of the constraint. Content should support real user questions, commercial pages and expertise rather than exist purely to increase article count.

Do you build backlinks?

Authority work may include link earning, digital PR, relevant mentions and local citations. The priority should be relevance and trust, not arbitrary link-volume targets.

Can you guarantee a #1 Google ranking?

No. Google controls its rankings. Ikon can commit to transparent implementation, evidence, measurement and accountable work — not a position no agency controls.

How do you report results?

Reporting combines what was implemented with Search Console, analytics, rankings or local evidence where useful, technical health and the next recommended actions.

What happens after I contact Ikon?

Start by sharing the website and the search problem or commercial goal. Ikon reviews the available evidence, identifies the highest-priority constraints and then recommends an appropriate scope.
