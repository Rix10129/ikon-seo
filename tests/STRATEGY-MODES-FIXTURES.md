# Strategy & Website Modes test fixtures

## Local business

Expected high-priority gaps when missing:

- Genuine service areas
- Verified phone or contact email
- Lead channels
- Local proof requirements
- Structured location record

Service and location pages should receive stronger business-value context than unrelated articles.

## Editorial publisher

Expected high-priority gaps when missing:

- Defined audience
- Primary topics
- Content hubs
- Author and reviewer policy
- Disclosure policy when affiliate, advertising or sponsorship monetisation is selected
- Originality standard

Posts aligned with declared topics should receive stronger business-value context than excluded topics.

## Ecommerce

Expected high-priority gaps when missing:

- Priority product categories
- Commerce conversion events
- Trust requirements
- Supported product data source

Products and categories should receive stronger business-value context than unrelated pages.

## Safety

- Reading the strategy must not alter settings.
- Saving requires `save=true` and draft access.
- Strategy changes must not invalidate the profile fingerprint or workflow key.
- Publishing, live edits, redirects, canonicals and external actions remain approval-gated.
- The focused OpenAPI schema must contain exactly 30 unique operations.
